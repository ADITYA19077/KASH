A Note That Took Its Time

Written in the quiet hours · For Kashish

Hi Kashish (itna suroor hai iss naam mein, wah bhai!),

Hehe, I started writing this and realised… it’s been a while, hasn’t it? You’ve been busy, and same here, mee tooooooo. Suno na, how have you been? Aapke texts nahi aate, toh samajh jaata hoon ki pressure toh bahut hoga. Aap waise bhi bahut strong ho, I’m sure sab kuch manage kar rahi hogi. But still, suno na, Miss Kitty Kashish… how have you been, really?

Let’s start with me. Hi, I’m Aditya Prakash, the same bura insaan jisne tumhe ghost kiya aur tumse baat karna band kar diya. Honestly, it was a bad call on my part, and I know that. Par aapne hi ek baar mujhe bataya tha na, ki kabhi-kabhi kuch cheezein humein itni zaroori lagne lagti hain ki unhe bachane ke liye hum sab kuch chhod dete hain. Aur phir bhi, end mein woh cheez chali hi jaati hai.

Kuch aisi hi kahaani meri bhi rahi. I lost a year of my life trying to protect a relationship that, eventually, didn’t work out. So, yeah. Us din gol gappe khaane ke baad, jab meri tabiyat kharaab hui, I blocked you on my phone. I wasn’t in the right state of mind, and honestly, I became delusional. Looking back, I know I made a mistake. Somewhere along the way, I ended up losing a genuinely good friend in you.

Jab mujhe apni galtiyon ka ehsaas hua, tab tak main job chhod chuka tha. Haath mein kuch khaas nahi tha, so main ghar wapas chala gaya aur ek saal wahin raha. Kuch paise save kiye the, toh thoda ghooma-phira bhi, but that wasn’t really the point. Phir CAT diya, padhai ki, aur MBA shuru ho gaya. Basically, ek saal ke liye I just disappeared. Aur abhi 19th October ko apna 25th birthday celebrate kiya, toh ussi din aapka text aaya: “How are you…!” What timing, right?

✦ ❦ ✦

As for me, I’m fine… or at least, trying to be. I think I’ve become a little more mature, and I’m trying to put as much of myself into my work as I can. I’m part of the placement committee, where I’m working towards helping others grow, and I’m also a part of the Spirituality group, where I try to guide people who might be feeling a little lost. So, life has been keeping me occupied, in its own way.

In the meantime, I saw your post, and honestly, I couldn’t be prouder of you. I really wish I could’ve been there to cheer you on and stand by your side, but right now, all I have to offer is a sincere sorry. I just want you to know that you’re one of the finest, craziest, and most talented souls I know. I genuinely hope people at MDI are treating you well, warna main gayab kar dunga unhe!

I won’t take much of your time, but I’d genuinely love for us to reconnect sometime soon. I miss that bright smile of yours and those endless conversations. I made a mistake, and somewhere along the way, I lost a good friend. I can’t undo that, but I can at least own up to it.

I hope you can find it in your heart to forgive me. And, as MBA grads, after all the pain, pressure, and sleepless nights life has put us through, I hope our paths cross again someday. Maybe we’ll get to pick up one of those endless conversations right where we left off.

Yours, with a sincere apology,Aditya

P.S. Book ka lock toh bas tumhe thoda sa puzzle dene ke liye tha. The apology was never meant to stay hidden. Thank you for opening it, Miss Kitty. ❦
